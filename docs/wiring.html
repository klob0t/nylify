RFID Cassette Player

Wiring the ESP32, RC522 and OLED

Eleven connections across two buses. Two of them are where almost every build of this goes wrong, so they're called out first.

Read this before you strip a wire

!

The RC522's RST goes to GPIO 27 — not GPIO 22

Nearly every RC522 tutorial puts RST on GPIO 22. On this build that pin is the OLED's SCL, and the two cannot share it. Follow a stock tutorial here and you get a dead reader and a dead screen, with nothing in the logs to explain why.

!

The RC522 runs on 3.3 V only

Its VCC pin is not 5 V tolerant. Connecting it to VIN is the single most common way people destroy one, and the damage is permanent. Use the 3V3 pin. The OLED is equally happy on 3.3 V, so nothing in this build touches VIN at all.

Check your OLED's pin order before connecting power

These modules ship with two different header layouts — GND VCC SCL SDA and VCC GND SCL SDA. The silkscreen is the only authority. Getting the first two backwards feeds reversed polarity straight into the panel and usually kills it.

Verify before you trust it

Wire it up, flash, then open the serial monitor at 115200 and type DIAG. It probes each bus separately, so a fault points at one module instead of leaving you guessing. 

> DIAG

=== wiring check ===============================

I2C bus (OLED)  SDA=21 SCL=22
  device at 0x3C  <- SSD1306 OLED

SPI bus (RC522)  SS=5 SCK=18 MOSI=23 MISO=19 RST=27
  VersionReg = 0x92  (MFRC522 v2.0 -- OK)
  antenna gain = 0x70 (max)
  hold a tag on the reader now; a UID should print below
================================================

[card] on  04A2B3C1

Two good lines and you're done: a device at 0x3C, and a version register of 0x91, 0x92 or 0x12. Anything else is diagnosed below.

When it doesn't work

What you seeWhat it means
VersionReg = 0x00 MISO is being held low — the reader isn't driving the bus at all. Usually no power, or MISO/MOSI swapped. Check VCC is on 3V3.
VersionReg = 0xFF MISO floating high — nothing is answering. Same causes as 0x00, and also what you get if the module was already killed by 5 V.
I²C: nothing found SDA and SCL swapped, or the OLED has no power. Both are on 3V3, never VIN.
Found 0x3D, not 0x3C A normal variant. Set OLED_I2C_ADDR to 0x3D in include/config.h and reflash.
Version OK, no tag reads An antenna range problem, not wiring. Bring the tag closer, and mount it against the inside face of the shell rather than deep inside it.
Works, then drops out Nearly always header pins pressed in rather than soldered. Both modules ship with loose headers.

Solder the headers

Both modules arrive with the pin strips unattached. Friction-fitting them into a breadboard mostly works, which is worse than not working — you get intermittent faults that look like software bugs. The RC522 in particular reads fine on the bench and then fails once it's inside a cassette shell.
